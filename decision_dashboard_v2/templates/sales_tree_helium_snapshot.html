Estimated trailing 30-day Amazon searches, reported Oct 17 → Oct 24:

The Oct 24 point is the latest weekly history available at retrieval. These overlapping estimates are market context, not our sessions or a count of searches in one week. The earlier Oct 10 → Oct 17 estimates dropped substantially.

Investigate how ad spend is used

Cycling socks, phrase target: 166 clicks, 21 attributed orders, $359.21 spend and $768.79 attributed sales (46.7% ACoS).

Road cycling socks men, broad target: 42 clicks, 3 attributed orders, $82.41 spend and $86.97 attributed sales (94.8% ACoS).

Oct 1–22 target-level results from search-term reports, reviewed Oct 30; attribution can still change. Attributed purchases may include other products. Confirm the current target, bid and advertised product before changes. ACoS is ad spend ÷ ad-attributed sales; TACOS uses total sales.

Takeaway: Estimated demand fell before the change and was broadly stable in the latest weekly estimate, while the tracked parent's organic rank improved on these terms. Traffic, conversion and ad efficiency all need attention. Consider testing relevant traffic within the existing budget, then judge the result by contribution after ads, three-pack sales and TACOS.
