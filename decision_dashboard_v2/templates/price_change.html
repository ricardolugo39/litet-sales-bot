How to read it

A shift toward larger packs with stable or higher total pairs and contribution may be a beneficial upsell. A shift that reduces total pairs or contribution may be harmful substitution.

These are hypotheses about aggregate mix, not proof that an individual customer switched packs. Price, volume, and mix move together; no causal credit is assigned.

Same-color/size movement is available in the expandable comparison above. The unresolved B0FFQ5Z8CS pack mapping limits pair conclusions.
