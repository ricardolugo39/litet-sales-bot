How to interpret the map

The two direct profit branches reconcile in dollars. Percentages use different baselines and cannot be added. Pack and traffic movements help identify where to investigate, but price, advertising, promotions and inventory can all change at once. A higher advertising cost is marked unfavorable in the profit bridge even though ads may support sales. Results are descriptive.

Sources: order records, product and cost ledgers, Amazon fee and economics records, brand advertising and Business Traffic snapshots. Fee estimates use available historical rates; released zero-fee records are treated as zero pending later adjustments. Results are estimates, not net profit. {% if not ad_complete %}Advertising is not loaded through the comparison end date; the profit bridge is withheld.{% endif %} Values may not add exactly after rounding.
